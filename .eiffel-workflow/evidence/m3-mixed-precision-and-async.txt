## 2. Asynchronous submission (simple_vulkan 0.7.0)
Finding: svk_flush submitted and then waited; auto-flush only every 16,384 commands; a step is
about 1,700 dispatches. So the host recorded a step with the GPU idle, then waited with itself
idle: wall = host + GPU.
Change: ring of 3 slots, submit every 128 commands without waiting; device-only barriers for
ordinary commands. Tests: simple_vulkan 18/18 (new async_submission), simple_sdf 26/26,
simple_tensor GPU 7/7 (bit-identical figures), simple_rixgpt_train 6/6.
